# PART 1 — THE MINDSET: GO FOR NO

> *"The goal is not to get a yes. The goal is to collect no's until a yes appears."*

Most salespeople quit after 2 rejections. Here's the truth:

| Attempt | % of Sales Closed |
|---------|------------------|
| After 1st contact | 2% |
| After 2nd contact | 3% |
| After 3rd contact | 5% |
| After 4th contact | 10% |
| After 5th+ contact | **80%** |

**The "Go for No" System:**
- Set a daily NO goal, not a yes goal. Example: *"Today I will get 10 no's."*
- Every no moves you mathematically closer to a yes.
- Rejection is data. It tells you what to refine, not to stop.
- The prospect who says no today may say yes in 30 days — follow up.
- Speed through no's. Don't try to save every call. Grade them and move.

**Your Daily Targets (starting point — adjust as you grow):**

| Activity | Daily Goal |
|----------|-----------|
| Outreach attempts (calls/DMs/walk-ins) | 20 |
| Conversations held | 8 |
| Pitches delivered | 4 |
| Discovery calls booked | 1–2 |
| No's collected | 10+ |

# PART 4 — THE SALES SCRIPT

## Core Philosophy
> Don't sell services. **Uncover their story. Then show them it deserves to be seen.**

The goal of every conversation is to make the prospect feel *heard* before you ever make an ask.

---

## OPENING

**Phone/Cold Call:**
> *"Hey [Name], this is [Your Name] with Moore Covenant Productions. I'm not calling to pitch you — I just want to ask you one quick question.*
>
> *What made you start your business?"*

**Walk-In/In-Person:**
> *"Hey, I love what you've built here. Quick question — what's the story behind this place? What made you start it?"*

**DM/Social Media:**
> *"Hey [Name] — came across your page and I love what you're doing. Real question: does your online presence actually reflect how good you are at what you do? Asking because we help businesses like yours make sure the answer is yes."*

**Then: shut up. Let them talk.**

This one question does three things:
1. Disarms them — you're not selling yet
2. Gets them emotionally connected to their own story
3. Gives you everything you need for the pitch

---

## DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

As they talk, guide with follow-ups:

> *"How long have you been doing this?"*

> *"Who's your ideal client — who do you do your best work for?"*

> *"Are you getting consistent leads from social media right now?"*

> *"If someone searched you online today — would what they find match what you just told me?"*

**That last question is the pivot.** Most will say no or hesitate. That's your opening.

---

## THE PITCH

> *"That's exactly why we exist.*
>
> *At Moore Covenant Productions, we don't just make content — we find the story behind what you built and we put it where the world can see it.*
>
> *Right now there are people who need exactly what you do. But they can't find you. Not because you're not good enough — because your story hasn't been told yet. The right way. In the right place. To the right people.*
>
> *We fix that. We sit with you, we learn what you're really about, and we build content that actually sounds like you. Not like every other business on Instagram. Your story is the most powerful marketing tool you have. We just help you tell it."*

---

## OBJECTION HANDLING

**"I've tried social media before and it didn't work."**
> *"I hear that all the time. Can I ask — was the content actually about your story, or was it just posts to fill a calendar? Most agencies give you content. We give you strategy built around who you actually are. Cookie-cutter gets cookie-cutter results. We don't do templates."*

**"I can't afford it right now."**
> *"I understand. Can I ask you this — what's it costing you to stay invisible? Every month without a real presence is a month someone else is getting the clients that should be yours. What if we found a starting point that fit where you are right now?"*

**"I need to think about it."**
> *"Absolutely — I respect that. Can I ask: what's the one thing you'd need to feel confident moving forward? Let me answer that right now so you have what you need to make the decision."*

**"I do my own content."**
> *"That's great — and you probably know how much time that takes. What we do is take that completely off your plate so you can stay in your zone. While you do what you do best, we make sure your story is reaching the right people, every single day, consistently."*

**"I already have someone doing my social media."**
> *"Good — that means you already believe in it. Can I ask: are you seeing real leads and growth from it, or is it more just posting to post? Because there's a difference between content and a content strategy. We'd love to show you what a story-first approach looks like."*

**"Send me some information."**
> *"I'll do that — and I'll keep it short. But before I do, can I ask you one more question: if your online presence truly reflected the quality of what you do, what would that mean for your business? I want to make sure what I send is actually relevant to where you want to go."*

---

## THE CLOSE

> *"Here's what I'd love to do — let's set up a Brand Story Call. No pressure, no pitch deck. Just 30 minutes where we learn about your business and show you exactly what your content presence could look like.*
>
> *If it makes sense, we move forward. If it doesn't, you walk away knowing your story better than you did before.*
>
> *Does [day] or [day] work better for you?"*

Always give **two specific days.** Not "does that work?" — two options forces a small commitment decision.

# PART 5 — CANVASSING METHODS BY CHANNEL

## Phone (Cold Calling)
- **Best hours:** Tue–Thu, 10am–12pm and 2pm–5pm
- Use the script above, opening question first
- If voicemail: *"Hey [Name], this is [Your Name] with Moore Covenant Productions. I came across your business and I had one quick question for you. Give me a call back at [number] — I'll keep it short."*
- Follow up 5–8 times before marking as dead

## In-Person / Walk-In Canvassing (DC, Virginia, Baltimore)
**Where to go:**
- Local business strips and corridors
- Barbershops, salons, beauty businesses
- Faith-based businesses and church-adjacent services
- Fitness studios, wellness centers
- Restaurants and food businesses with a story
- Real estate offices
- Financial advisors and coaches

**How to walk in:**
> *"Hey, I'm [Name] — I work with a local media company called Moore Covenant Productions. We help small businesses tell their story online. I just had one quick question for the owner — is he or she around?"*

Then use the script.

## Social Media / DM Outreach
- Lead with a genuine observation about their content
- Never copy-paste the same DM — personalize the first line
- Use the DM opening from the script above
- Follow up 3 days later if no response

## Referral / Network
- Ask every closed client: *"Who's one person in your network who has a great story but no one can see it online?"*
- Every referral is a warm intro — the script becomes a conversation, not a pitch
